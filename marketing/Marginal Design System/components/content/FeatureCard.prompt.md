Feature tile for the marketing grid. One idea per card, two sentences maximum.

```jsx
<FeatureCard glyph="##" title="Marks hide themselves"
  body="Syntax markers fade out as you type and reappear only around the cursor."
  shortcut={<Shortcut keys={['⌘','1']} />} />
```

Icons are monospace glyphs from markdown itself, not pictograms.
